Bhargav B S

Researcher, analyst, and writer working between data, fieldwork, and public argument.

Hello. I am currently a Research Associate at the Centre for the Study of the Indian Economy, Azim Premji University. My work sits between development economics, social policy, and public communication. I care about how welfare systems actually work in people's lives, and I like building research that is rigorous, readable, and useful outside the university.

I am especially interested in work that needs both analytical care and narrative judgement: projects where the point is not only to produce findings, but to make them intelligible to policymakers, researchers, journalists, NGOs, and the people affected by them.
